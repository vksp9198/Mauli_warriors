ABOUT US

Mauli Warriors is a group that works towards the organization and holding of different esports events. Now, Mauli Warriors has its eyes fixated on smooth-running operational procedures, as well as delightful experiences. Our participants have nothing to worry about starting from registration to game play to post-event activities

Esports in India is a rapidly growing industry, fueled by a large and engaged mobile gaming community and increasing government recognition. It's transforming into a significant cultural and economic force, attracting intemational attention and investment

Exports are important because they offer various benefits, including skill development, social interaction, and potential career opportunities. They also contribute to a growing industry with significant economic impact and are increasingly recognized as a legitimate form of competition
